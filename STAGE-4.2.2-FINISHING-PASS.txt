BlueCircuit Electronics — Stage 4.2.2 finishing pass

Changes:
- Fixed the category mega-menu hover presentation and visible chevrons; subcategory links and parent-product/group links are shown in the wide hover panel.
- Added a fourth footer newsletter subscription card and a safe subscription endpoint.
- Made the complete breadcrumb path (links, separators and current product) orange in light and dark themes.
- Expanded the Add/Edit Product form with parent-category, subcategory and optional nested-category selectors. The category selection is dependent. It also supports creating a parent category, subcategory and hidden parent-product grouping from the product form.
- Added favicon upload and preview in Admin > Store Settings. The storefront automatically emits the favicon <link> tag in its document head; the generated example is displayed in settings.

Installation:
1. Back up your current project and database.
2. Copy the contents of this package over your existing electronics-store folder. Preserve config/database.php and existing uploads.
3. In phpMyAdmin, select electronics_store and run database/migrations/20261103_newsletter_subscribers.sql once.
4. No other migration is required for these changes. The parent_product_id and category.parent_id structure is part of the earlier Stage 4.2 migrations.
5. Clear browser cache or hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5).

Notes:
- New parent-product groups created from the product form are saved as inactive placeholder products so they do not appear as purchasable stock items. Their names appear in the relevant mega-menu; associated sellable products can be assigned to the group.
- Newsletter subscriptions are stored in newsletter_subscribers. No mailing service is connected; this records subscribers for later export/marketing integration.
- PHP syntax and ZIP integrity were checked. A live XAMPP/database/browser integration test was not possible in this environment.
